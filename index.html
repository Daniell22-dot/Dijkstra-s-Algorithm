About The Project

Final Year Project Overview

This project is developed as part of the final year requirements for the Institute of Geomatics Enginering, Remote sensing and Geospatial Information Sytems at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology.

The system demonstrates the integration of Building Information Modeling (BIM) from ArchiCAD with Geographic Information Systems (GIS) to create an interactive web-based 3D campus digital twin with indoor routing capabilities.
